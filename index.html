Settings / Help

The positions

  • Marshal Red frequency. Checks arriving aircraft in, assigns marshal holding (radial, DME, angels) and expected approach times.
  • Approach Silver frequency. Aircraft launch from the ship; you take radar contact, vector them, pass traffic and hand them to Final.
  • Final Purple / Copper frequencies. Precision approach (PAR): course and glidepath calls down to the deck.
  • Instructor Watches and commands the aircraft on the other scopes, pauses the sim, cuts radar and chooses when the next aircraft checks in.

Linking positions

Open several positions in separate tabs or windows on the same device and browser. Choose Link ON on each start screen and they share traffic: Marshal or Approach hands aircraft to Final, and they all use the same BRC. Open the Instructor in another tab and pick Marshal, Approach or both; Final is always linked to it.

Voice and microphone

  • Use Chrome (desktop or Android) or Safari (iPhone / iPad / Mac). Other browsers may not support speech recognition.
  • Allow the microphone when the browser asks. If you blocked it, re-enable it in the site settings (the lock or "aA" icon in the address bar).
  • Push to talk: hold SPACE or the big mic button, speak the whole call, then release.
  • Speak at a normal pace in a quiet room. Every scope also accepts typed commands.

Shorthand typing

In a scope's command box, type ? and press Enter to see the shorthand cheat sheet for that position.

Data blocks (Marshal & Approach)

Accepted aircraft get a letter in the order you accept them, shown with a short callsign (A LT21). Type the letter first to talk to it: A h270. Ground speed is hidden at the start of each sim; type gsp once and it shows on every data block, including aircraft that arrive later. Type gsp again to hide it. One arrival per sim shows up as a primary target only: click it, then sqn (squawk normal) or sq4521 brings its data block back.

FAQ / troubleshooting

How do I get the latest version?

Refresh the page (pull down on iPhone / iPad, or Ctrl+R / Cmd+R). The app always loads the newest files when you are online.

Something still looks old or broken

Open Settings → Clear app cache and reload. If that does not help, use Reset all saved trainer data.

Positions are not linking

All positions must be open in the same browser on the same device, with Link ON chosen on each start screen. Reload a tab that shows "not detected".

Add it to my iPhone / iPad home screen

In Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Each position opens full screen like an app.

The microphone does not work

Check that the browser has microphone permission, that no other app is using the mic, and that you are using Chrome or Safari. You can always type the call instead.
