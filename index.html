Amphibious air traffic control training

Radar scopes for practicing AATCC aboard USS Wasp: check aircraft in at Marshal, vector them on Approach and talk them down on the Final PAR, by voice or by typing. Open each position in its own tab and link them to run a full recovery together.

A training aid. Not for operational use.
