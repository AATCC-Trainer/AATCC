Precision Approach Radar talk-down simulator · Runway 18

You're the final controller. Each aircraft appears on your scope 7 to 9 miles out. Using your voice, talk it down: heading corrections, course and glidepath calls, range calls, the gear check and the landing clearance, all the way to decision height. Pilots answer each call with their callsign until you say "do not acknowledge further transmissions"; after that they stay quiet, so keep talking!
